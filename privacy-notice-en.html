Privacy Notice

How we use and protect your data when you book with WalkMe.

Data controller. WalkMe Travel ("WalkMe"), with service address at 5th Ave between 10th and 12th St, Playa del Carmen, Quintana Roo, Mexico, email walkmetravel@gmail.com, is responsible for the processing of your personal data under Mexico's Federal Law on Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties (LFPDPPP).

Data we collect for your booking. Full name, email, phone number, hotel or meeting point, a photo of your official ID, your lodging confirmation and your electronic signature of the services agreement.

Purposes. We use your data solely to: (1) manage and confirm your booking with the operators of each experience, (2) process your payment and issue your confirmation voucher, (3) contact you by email or WhatsApp about your booking, and (4) comply with legal obligations. We do not use your data for advertising, nor sell or share it with third parties unrelated to your booking.

Safekeeping. Your documents are stored encrypted in a private repository; only WalkMe staff can access them through temporary links. Payments are processed directly by Mercado Pago or Stripe: WalkMe never sees or stores your card details.

Retention. Your ID photo and lodging confirmation are deleted within 30 days after your tour. The signed agreement and booking data are kept for as long as the law requires.

Your rights (ARCO). You may request access, rectification, cancellation of, or objection to the processing of your data by writing to walkmetravel@gmail.com or via WhatsApp at +52 56 3974 8122. We will respond within the timeframes set by the LFPDPPP.

Changes. Any update to this notice will be published on this page. Last updated: August 2026.
