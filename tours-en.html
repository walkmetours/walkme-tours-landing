Contoy Island · Isla Mujeres

Paradise Island

Visit Contoy, the last unspoiled island of the Mexican Caribbean, where no one lives. Snorkel one of the largest coral reefs in the world, enjoy a meal cooked on the spot by the crew, and finish with free time in Isla Mujeres.

DurationFull day
Days (2026)Every day except Thursday
AgeKids 4 to 11 pay the child rate
DepartureHotel pickup

Itinerary

  • 6:00 to 9:00 · Pickup at your hotel lobby, with a light breakfast (cereal bar, cookies, banana, juice and coffee)
  • 9:30 · Boat departure from Punta Sam toward the reef
  • 10:00 · Snorkeling on the Contoy reserve's coral reef, weather permitting
  • 11:20 · Arrival and optional tour of Isla Contoy
  • 12:00 · Meal: tikinxic fish and grilled chicken, with soft drinks and beer
  • 2:30 pm · Sailing to Isla Mujeres
  • 3:00 pm · One hour of free time on Isla Mujeres
  • 4:30 pm · Back to the pier and your hotel

What's included

  • Air-conditioned transportation
  • Bilingual guide
  • Breakfast at the pier
  • Meal on the island
  • Water, soft drinks and beer (beer from 12:30 pm)
  • All snorkel gear

Not included

  • Federal marina and national park fee: 20 USD per person, cash

What to bring

  • Towel
  • Cool, comfortable clothes, a long-sleeve shirt
  • A change of clothes
  • Swimsuit
  • Hat or cap
  • Sunglasses
  • Sandals
  • Cash for photos, souvenirs and tips

FAQ

What food is included?

A light breakfast at the pier and a meal on the island: tikinxic fish, grilled chicken, rice, salad, chips and salsas, with soft drinks and beer.

Can I go ashore on Isla Contoy?

Yes, there's an optional guided walk; it's a protected nature reserve.

What happens if the weather is bad?

Reef snorkeling depends on the weather; the captain makes the call for safety.
