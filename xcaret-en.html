questions?

Why book with WalkMe instead of directly on Xcaret's official page?

We're official allies of Grupo Xcaret and we're proud to work with them. We charge the same price as the official page, plus personal attention that's hard to get from such a large company. Most bookings are paid through a secure, simple payment link; if you'd feel more confident, we can also meet you at your hotel to deliver your tickets and collect payment in person, though that's not something we do for every booking. On top of that, we give you personalized advice to plan your day, tips to enjoy it more, and we make sure you catch your transportation if you booked it with us.

Where is Xcaret and how far is it from Playa del Carmen?

Xcaret is in the Riviera Maya, very close to Playa del Carmen: just about 15 minutes by car.

Can I get to Xcaret on an electric bike?

Yes, and it's very safe! There's a dedicated bike path all the way to Xcaret, built by the park itself, so it's not just safe, it's also a really fun way to get there. We highly recommend it.

Can I pay in US dollars?

Yes, for cash payments. Card payments can only be charged in Mexican pesos.

How safe is it to pay online?

There's a lot of fraud online, so it's worth researching any agency before paying. We offer secure platforms like PayPal and Mercado Pago, where you can file a claim and cancel the payment if we don't deliver what we offered.

What are the best Xcaret parks?

It comes down to taste: some parks are about adventure, others about connecting with nature. Xcaret stands out for its mix of nature, culture and surprises. If you're after thrills, Xplor or Xplor Fuego are great; Xenses is perfect for new sensory experiences, and Xel-Há is ideal for a full all-inclusive day in the Riviera Maya.

Which parks are good for kids?

Xcaret and Xel-Há are the most family-friendly: they have rivers, calm snorkeling, waterslides, and activities for all ages. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll help you choose based on your kids' ages.

Is the night show worth it on its own?

Yes, it's the best show in the Riviera Maya. You shouldn't miss it.

Isn't it too artificial?

No. Xcaret has very high standards, including endangered species breeding programs and projects with biologists, among many other initiatives.

Is Xcaret worth visiting?

100%, yes. It's like going to Paris and not seeing the Eiffel Tower, or going to Orlando and not visiting Disney.

Xcaret Plus

Xcaret is the flagship park of the Riviera Maya: nature, Mexican culture and adventure in a single day. The Plus wristband adds a buffet meal, an exclusive area and snorkel gear.

DurationFull day
Hours8:30 to 22:30 approx.
AgeAll ages
DepartureFrom Playa del Carmen

What's included

  • Underground rivers and snorkeling in the cove
  • Aviary, butterfly pavilion and reef aquarium
  • Mayan village, hacienda and cemetery
  • Xcaret México Espectacular night show, with over 300 performers
  • Buffet and exclusive Plus area with snorkel gear

Not included

  • Professional photos and videos
  • Transport (we quote it separately for your area)

FAQ

What is the difference between Xcaret and Xcaret Plus?

The Plus wristband includes a buffet meal, an exclusive area with a view, lockers and snorkel gear. Basic access lets you enjoy every attraction, but without those extras.

How much time do I need for Xcaret?

A full day is ideal. It opens at 8:30 in the morning and the night show ends around 22:30, so it is best to arrive early.

Is it good for kids?

Yes, it is one of the most complete parks for families: calm rivers, gentle snorkeling and activities for all ages.
